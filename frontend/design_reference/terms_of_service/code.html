01

Nature of the Platform (Introductions Only)

The Founder's Carnet exists solely to facilitate curated, bilateral mutual-consent introductions between validated founders, senior tech talent, seasoned mentors, and institutional or angel investors operating across Pakistan's technology ecosystems.

Explicit Negative Covenants // We do NOT:

  • close Provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advisory of any form.
  • close Act as a broker-dealer, funding portal, crowdfunding platform, or statutory placement agent.
  • close Charge transaction commissions, participate in carried interest, take equity positions, or guarantee funding outcomes.
  • close Guarantee direct employment, recruitment placements, advisory remuneration, or commercial success.
02

Eligibility & Verification Standards

Membership accounts are strictly reserved for operational early-stage venture founders, high-agency engineering and executive operators, domain mentors, and vetted angel syndicates or fund managers actively engaged with Pakistan's regional technology hubs (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and regional corridors).

verified_user Dual-Layer Vetting

Every applicant dossier undergoes combined algorithmic integrity parsing alongside discreet peer review by ecosystem fellows before admission to the registry.

gavel Single Appeal Rule

If an application is declined, the candidate is entitled to exactly one structured reconsideration petition with supplementary evidence. Decisions after this single review are permanent.

03

Mutual-Consent Code of Conduct

Ecosystem integrity rests upon courteous, consensual communications. Members agree to honor bilateral communication boundaries without exception:

  • Absolute Prohibition on Cold Outbound: Unsolicited pitch mass-mailers, untargeted outreach, and attempts to circumvent the carnets bilateral hand-shake system are strictly disallowed.
  • Bilateral Opt-In: Direct coordinates (email, phone, personal messaging channels) are only decrypted and transmitted once both parties explicitly confirm mutual willingness to converse. A non-response or passive pass must be treated with immediate professional grace.
  • Veracity of Representations: Material falsification of company runway, annualized run-rates (ARR), cap table claims, employment records, or academic background triggers instant, permanent account cancellation without notice.
04

The Readiness Score Rubric

The 7-dimension venture assessment score generated through the platform is an educational diagnostic benchmark. It serves solely to pinpoint structural operational blindspots, narrative inconsistencies, and readiness gaps before engaging sophisticated partners.

info

The score is not an algorithmic guarantee, creditworthiness ranking, investment scorecard, or statistical predictor of future capital allocation.

05

Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

Founders retain complete, undivided 100% intellectual property rights over all decks, proprietary financial forecasts, data room archives, software repositories, and venture assets uploaded to their workspace. The platform asserts zero equity interest, commercial license, or residual claim over member works.

06

Account Termination & Self-Erasure

Members maintain full sovereignty over their tenancy. You may close your membership file at any moment via the identity control pane inside your dashboard. Account self-erasure is permanent and irrevocably purges all rubric benchmarks, mock pitch transcripts, peer annotations, and pending bilateral introductions.

07

Platform Availability & Disclaimers

The Founder's Carnet is provided strictly on an "as is" and "as available" basis. While we maintain rigorous data protection and high uptime infrastructure, we disclaim all warranties, express or statutory, regarding uninterrupted availability, marketability, or fitness for particular fundraising campaigns.

08

Inquiries & Contact

Clarifications regarding these mutual terms, requests for legal clarification, or reports of code of conduct violations should be submitted via the platform concierge desk or routed directly to our registrar.
