Profile verification states

Profiles submitted across Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad undergo automated data checks followed by an objective peer reviewer. Below are the three post-submission screens shown as standalone frames.

Frame 01 State: Under review
Current status

Under review

Your profile is currently with a reviewer

Automated checks on your details are complete. A member of the network team is now conducting a human review to verify ecosystem fit.

What happens next

  • 01

    Human review

    We evaluate startup links, sector experience, and track record. Most reviews finish within 24 hours.

  • 02

    Email notification

    You will receive an email as soon as a determination is recorded on your profile.

  • 03

    Mutual-consent introductions

    Once approved, your profile becomes visible and you may request introductions. Contact details remain hidden until both parties accept.

Need to update information before review finishes? Contact the review desk directly.

You can appeal once. A rejection after the appeal is final.

Before submitting an appeal, please have direct links to verifiable artifacts ready (such as an active company demo, public GitHub repo, or verifiable press feature).
