Exchange 01 Opening Thesis & Customer Pain
FH Faraz Haider (Investor):

“Bilal, how does Munshi Books prevent the informal paper khata fraud that usually breaks digital ledger trials in wholesale markets?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“We eliminate single-party record tampering through dual-entry WhatsApp ledger confirmation. When a wholesaler logs an invoice, the recipient confirms via one-click WhatsApp prompt in Urdu with receipt snapshot reconciliation. Disputed receivables plummet by 42% in month one.”

Exchange 02 Unit Economics & Payback
FH Faraz Haider (Investor):

“Let's examine your customer acquisition cost. How do you maintain a blended CAC low enough to achieve venture-grade payback in Karachi?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Our blended CAC stands at PKR 2,400. Merchants pay an upfront recurring subscription of PKR 1,800/month plus nominal reconciliation batch fees, yielding full capital payback in 3.4 months across dense market clusters.”

Exchange 03 Defensibility vs Commercial Banks
FH Faraz Haider (Investor):

“What stops major players like HBL or Easypaisa from distributing identical free digital invoicing tools and crushing your adoption?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Commercial banks deliver basic payment settlement rails, not deep merchant inventory workflows. Munshi Books integrates multi-tier wholesale credit trees, partial returns, and informal grain khata customs. Merchants trust our localized privacy model over direct bank reporting.”

Exchange 04 Merchant Retention
FH Faraz Haider (Investor):

“How does merchant churn behave during seasonal liquidity swings, particularly around pre-Eid cash crunches?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Our 6-month cohort retention is 82% in Boulton Market and Jodia Bazaar. Retention actually strengthens during seasonal volume spikes because daily ledger transparency prevents duplicate payment disputes during heavy festive inventory turnover.”

Exchange 05 Invoice Discounting Working Capital
FH Faraz Haider (Investor):

“As you launch invoice discounting, what prevents default cascades when regional supply chains freeze or receivables stretch past 45 days?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“We enforce strict 30-day limits and require wholesale guild cross-guarantees. We advance only up to 70% of verified invoices backed by verified historical counterparty trade receipts, preserving an escrow buffer against duration risk.”

Exchange 06 Regional Expansion to Punjab
FH Faraz Haider (Investor):

“Can you realistically scale into Punjab (Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad) without recreating high field operations overhead?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“We acknowledge merchant credit habits in Punjab have distinct mandi settlement dynamics. Rather than bloated direct sales armies, our expansion playbook partners directly with grain and yarn trade associations through authorized local market agents.”

Exchange 07 Regulatory & Tax Integration

“Does your automated sales tax module create friction with merchants terrified of FBR digital audits?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“We provide standard exportable accounting sheets without automated sovereign filing or legal tax endorsement. The merchant retains sovereign control over data export, removing any fear of unsolicited regulatory exposure.”
