Exchange 01 Opening Thesis
AQ Ayesha Qureshi (Investor):

“Walk me through Munshi Books in 30 seconds. What is the acute pain in Pakistani wholesale trade?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Over 80% of wholesale retail in Karachi runs on informal paper credit (khata). We provide an automated bookkeeping and sales tax ledger in Urdu that integrates directly with merchant WhatsApp and supplier ledgers, reducing disputed balances by 42%.”

Exchange 02 Moat & Defensibility
AQ Ayesha Qureshi (Investor):

“How do you defend margins when major commercial banks are offering free digital merchant QR and invoicing tools?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Banks offer commodity settlement, not merchant workflow. Our software embeds into supplier inventory reconciliations and khata balances. The switching cost is high because 14 active wholesale corridors rely on our automated weekly ledger settlement.”

Exchange 03 Acquisition & Geography
AQ Ayesha Qureshi (Investor):

“What is your current CAC and how does that hold as you expand outside Karachi into Punjab?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Our blended CAC in Karachi is PKR 2,400, recovered in 3.4 months via monthly SaaS subscription plus transaction reconciliations. For Punjab, we are currently testing trade association distribution, though we have not yet quantified bottom-up TAM for regional grain markets.”

Exchange 04 TAM Defense & SOM
AQ Ayesha Qureshi (Investor):

“That brings us to market sizing. You cited a $2B GCC and Pakistan opportunity, but what is the immediate serviceable market?”

BA Bilal Ahmed (Founder):

“Our immediate SOM is 120,000 formalizing FMCG and wholesale traders in Karachi, Lahore, and Faisalabad. We acknowledge our regional mandi calculations need more granularity, which we are currently modeling.”
