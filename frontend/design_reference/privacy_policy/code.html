Carnet Covenant // In Brief

The Founder’s Carnet operates on strict bilateral privacy: no member receives unsolicited phone calls, public email scraping is architecturally blocked, and sensitive pitch assets are shared only when both sides explicitly accept an introduction.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

Overview & Core philosophy

The Founder’s Carnet is engineered strictly as a mutual-consent introduction engine for Pakistan’s frontier innovation landscape. We operate neither as an open marketplace broker nor as a public corporate registry.

We do not sell personal data, monetize contact directories, syndicate cap tables, or allow passive portfolio harvesting. Every transaction across this system is governed by a singular protocol: coordinate data remains encrypted and shielded until both founders and capital stewards explicitly consent to talk.

02 / TAXONOMY

Information We collect

We collect only the bare computational parameters required to execute high-fidelity, high-conviction introductions between venture teams and sector champions.

  • Primary operational role

03 / PROTOCOL

The Mutual-Consent Disclosure model

Unlike public directories or unsolicited cold-pitch platforms, data disclosure on The Founder’s Carnet progresses along a three-tier cryptographic gate. No party may harvest another’s coordinates unilaterally.

01

Public & Network Index

Sanitized Layer

Only high-level architectural summaries, sector badges, and normalized pitch rubric benchmarks are queryable. Direct work email addresses, telephone numbers, and sensitive slide financials are NEVER displayed on platform feeds or search outputs.

02

Introduction Request

Bilateral Handshake Pending

When a founder or partner requests an introduction, the counterpart receives only an anonymized thesis fit breakdown and a single-line editorial rationale. Direct contact identities remain completely locked inside platform escrow.

03

Bilateral Acceptance

Coordinates Released

Direct communication channels (verified email and telegram/cal link) unlock exclusively after BOTH entities press “Accept”. If either counterparty declines, archives, or fails to engage, all direct coordinates stay permanently sequestered.

04 / MACHINE INTELLIGENCE

AI Pitch Simulations & transcripts

When founders utilize our rehearsal suite to simulate rigorous LP or venture interrogation, all synthetic Q&A sessions, audio fragments, and diagnostic scores are quarantined within an isolated founder workspace.

These simulation artifacts exist exclusively for your internal revision. They are never ingested into global training weights, never accessible to prospective investors, and never syndicated to advisory members without your unmistakable affirmative command.

05 / SOVEREIGNTY

Data Retention & Permanent deletion

Founders and operators retain absolute custody of their digital records. You may trigger an unconditional platform purge through your account cockpit at any moment.

Purge Execution Mechanics

Upon confirmation, your pitch documents, draft metrics, AI diagnostic sessions, and pending invitation loops are permanently purged from active and warm-cache infrastructure within 72 hours.

Please note: Introductions that reached Stage 3 bilateral consummation exist in the external mail records of the accepting parties and cannot be retroactively retracted.

06 / TELEMETRY

Cookies & Technical logs

Our telemetry footprint is deliberately minimal. We set only strictly necessary HTTP session credentials required for cryptographic authentication and cross-site token protection. We run zero behavioral advertising trackers, zero social pixel beacons, and no algorithmic cross-site retargeting scripts.
