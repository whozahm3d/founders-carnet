01 / Foundation

Finding the right people should not depend on who you already know.

The closed circle challenge

In emerging ecosystems like Pakistan, early-stage ventures often rely heavily on closed informal networks and social circles. High-potential founders, engineers, and specialists outside existing cliques frequently remain overlooked simply because they lack an introductory bridge.

Objective venture profiles

The Founder's Carnet establishes a level playing field. We build objective, verified profiles, provide founders with a clear 7-dimension readiness rubric, evaluate similarity across operational focus, and facilitate direct, bilateral introductions without gatekeepers.

What we strictly do not do

We are not a brokerage, funding portal, or placement firm. We take zero transaction fees, do not deal in equity, provide no legal or tax advice, and strictly enforce a zero-tolerance policy against cold outreach spam.

05 / Ethics

Our core principles

The founder ecosystem is built on trust, confidentiality, and objective standards rather than vanity hype.

01

A rubric guide, not a funding probability

The 7-dimension readiness rubric provides objective, operational diagnostics. It is not an algorithmic promise or speculative predictor of venture investment outcomes.

02

Profile similarity, not likelihood of success

Introductions are suggested when sector focus, stage, business model, and operational milestones align between both parties—never based on subjective favoritism.

03

Contact details after mutual acceptance

Cold unsolicited direct messages are impossible by architecture. Emails and telephone coordinates remain strictly encrypted until both participants formally opt in.

04

Human review for unclear profiles; appeal once

When automated background checks are inconclusive, profiles undergo human review by vetted industry peers. A rejection is final after one structured appeal to safeguard community integrity.

06 / Inquiry

Frequently asked questions

We accept verified Pakistani startup founders with registered legal entities, vetted tech operators, and active institutional or angel investors. General solicitations and recruiters are strictly outside our mandate.

Automated checks first validate work histories, company registration records, and corporate emails. Unresolved cases pass to a confidential peer panel of experienced operators within two business days.

Direct email addresses, telephone numbers, and scheduling links remain strictly encrypted until both participants formally accept the intro request. Either participant may decline discreetly without alerts or awkwardness.

We are strictly an introduction network, not a broker-dealer, accelerator, or placement agency. We do not participate in financial transactions, hold escrow, take closing fees, or demand startup equity.

Applicants receive transparent diagnostic notes detailing unverified claims. You are entitled to exactly one structured appeal with supplementary proof to ensure community integrity.
