Registration for PSEB Membership

Required documents for new companies and firms:
- Business NTN
- Business bank account opening or maintenance certificate
- CNIC (both sides) of owners, stakeholders, or partners
- Passport of owners, stakeholders, or partners who are foreign nationals
- Partnership deed for an association of persons
- Form C for registered firms only

Renewal of companies and firms:
- The latest tax return (Form 114 of FBR) is required only.

Membership fees for companies and firms:
- New registration, established within the first 12 months: Rs. 5,000
- New registration, established for more than 12 months: Rs. 10,000
- Renewal, revenue from Rs. 0 to 50 million: Rs. 10,000
- Renewal, revenue from Rs. 50 to 100 million: Rs. 15,000
- Renewal, revenue from Rs. 100 to 300 million: Rs. 20,000
- Renewal, revenue from Rs. 300 to 600 million: Rs. 25,000
- Renewal, revenue of Rs. 600 million and above: Rs. 30,000
- Late-renewal surcharge, if applied: Rs. 5,000

Required documents for call centers:
- Business NTN
- Business bank account opening or maintenance certificate
- CNIC (both sides) of owners, stakeholders, or partners
- Passport of owners, stakeholders, or partners who are foreign nationals
- Partnership deed for an association of persons
- Form C for registered firms only

Renewal of call centers:
- The latest tax return (Form 114 of FBR) is required only.

Membership fees for call centers:
- Main office, new or renewal, per year: Rs. 20,000
- Each branch office, new or renewal, per year: Rs. 10,000
- Late-renewal surcharge, if applied: Rs. 10,000

Required documents for freelancers:
- Personal NTN (with no business)
- Personal bank account opening or maintenance certificate
- Individual's CNIC

Renewal of freelancers:
- The latest tax return (Form 114 of FBR) is required only.

Membership fees for freelancers:
- New registration: Rs. 1,000
- Renewal: Rs. 2,000

Payment methods:
- Debit or credit card through the PSEB portal.
- Pay order from any bank. The page lists the beneficiary address as 6th Floor, State Life Tower, Jinnah Avenue, Block L, F-7/4, Blue Area, Islamabad, Islamabad Capital Territory. It lists NTN 2315376.

Processing and renewal information:
- Registration of SECP-registered companies or call centers takes approximately 9 minutes.
- Registration of non-SECP-registered entities, including freelancers and non-registered call centers, takes up to one working day after submission.
- A member's original registration date remains the same when a certificate is issued. Renewal updates only the year. Late renewal does not change the certificate duration.
