## Sources to cite (not datasets)

- **Mustafa et al. IT Job Market Preprint:** Mustafa, H., Faisal, M., & Ahmed, W. (2026). *Analysing IT Job Market Dynamics in Pakistan*. Preprints.org, 10 Sep 2026. DOI: [10.20944/preprints202609.0274.v2](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202609.0274.v2). Licence: CC BY 4.0. Note: Non-peer-reviewed preprint; covers 3,734 LinkedIn IT postings collected 2023-09-26 to 2023-11-26 (Lahore 1,198, Karachi 858, Islamabad 720; 31 internships). Do not cite abstract claim of inverse volume-competition relationship (Section IV-F finds r = -0.114, p = 0.614) or province table (sums to 3,697).
- **Dealroom Pakistan Ecosystem Page:** Dealroom.co (2026). *Pakistani Tech Ecosystem*. Available at: <https://dealroom.co/countries/pakistan/>. Public summary reports 26 Pakistan-born founders behind 22 unicorns (82% built in the US); full data is paid proprietary.
- **i2i Ventures Startup Funding Report:** i2i Ventures (2026). *Pakistan Startup Funding H1 2026* (published 2026-07-02). Available at: <https://i2iventures.substack.com/p/pakistan-startup-funding-h1-2026>. Reports USD 59M venture capital raised in Pakistan across H1 2026.
- **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025:** Stack Exchange Inc. (2025). *Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025*. Available at: <https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/developers>. Licence: ODbL. Includes 238 Pakistani developer respondents.
- **Butt et al. Roman Urdu IR dataset:** Butt, U., Varanasi, S., & Neumann, G. (2025). *Roman Urdu as a Low-Resource Language: Building the First IR Dataset and Baseline*. Proceedings of the 1st Workshop on Advancing NLP for Low-Resource Languages, pp. 82-87, Varna, Bulgaria. ACL Anthology 2025.lowresnlp-1.9, PDF: <https://preview.aclanthology.org/old-master/2025.lowresnlp-1.9.pdf>. Read 2026-10-18: builds the Roman Urdu MS MARCO (IndicTrans2 translation into Urdu, then transliteration); reports BM25 MRR@10 0.0846 against 0.1903 for a fine-tuned mT5 reranker on 6,980 queries; states no licence.
- **Butt et al. transliteration paper:** Butt, U., Varanasi, S., & Neumann, G. (2025). *Low-Resource Transliteration for Roman-Urdu and Urdu Using Transformer-Based Models*. arXiv:2503.21530. Describes Roman-Urdu-Parl as "collected from various informal online sources"; no licence stated.
